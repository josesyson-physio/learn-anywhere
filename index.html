      Why this exists

      Good coaching and books cost money many students do not have. Learn Anywhere gives every physiotherapy student the same quality of explanation, for free, on any phone.

      How answers are built. Choose your university, year, question type and marks. The answer follows the layout Indian BPT and MPT examiners expect: definition, pathophysiology, clinical features, assessment, phased management, diagram and conclusion. Advanced sections add evidence grades, outcome measures with clinically important change scores, recent trials and viva questions.

      Marking guide. The marks split is a typical pattern for Indian health science university papers. Always check your own university's syllabus and previous question papers.

      References. Library topics are original summaries that point to standard textbooks (see the Reference shelf in the Library) and to real guidelines and trials you can open on PubMed. No book text is copied. Always read the chapter for depth.

      Easy on the eyes. On the Ask screen you can switch on E-ink paper mode (soft grey paper, no colour or animation) and make the text bigger. Open any answer and press Focus mode to hide everything else while you read.

      Free forever. No account, no fees, no ads. The library grows topic by topic.

      Low network. The library and quiz are inside the page. A new answer uses only a few kilobytes of text. You can download any answer, or the whole library, as a PDF to read offline.

      Why this exists. Education should never stop at a barrier: money, distance, language, a slow network or a missed class. The animation at the top shows it: light from one book gets past a barrier, splits, and reaches every person, who together make the plus. Knowledge grows when it is shared.

      Use it fairly. Use it to learn, revise and teach, and share it freely. The content licence (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0) does not allow selling it or putting it behind a paywall. Please also do not copy from it during an exam, or use it to treat patients without a qualified clinician. The "Understand it like a friend" examples are for understanding only: write the proper terms in your exam.

      Built to help you remember. Every subject has its own colour and picture, shown on its cards and at the top of every answer, so your visual memory links them. Each topic shows its memory key word large. The daily goal (3 topics), study streak, levels and "Mark as learned" ticks keep you coming back. After 25 minutes the app reminds you to take a short break, and late at night it reminds you that sleep is when memory is saved. Your progress stays on your phone only.

      Independent. Learn Anywhere is an independent study aid. It is not affiliated with, approved or endorsed by any university, NIEPID, textbook publisher, Physiopedia, NICE, WHO or any other body named here. University names, syllabus outlines and book titles are used only to tell you what a topic covers and where to read more; their names and trademarks belong to their owners. Syllabus lines are short summaries, so always check the official syllabus for your batch.

      Your profile. Your name and optional 4-digit PIN exist only on this phone, so each person sharing a phone keeps their own streak. There is no email, no server account and no way for us to see or recover them. The PIN is stored only as a scrambled code. It just keeps profiles apart on a shared phone and is not a security lock, so never use your bank or phone PIN, or a password you use anywhere else. Forgot it? Choose "Forgot PIN?" and type the profile name to remove it and start again, then restore your progress file if you saved one. Switch profiles from the top of the home screen.

      Privacy. No ads, no cookies and nothing that identifies you. Your profile, progress and settings are saved only in this browser on your phone, and clearing site data deletes them. We collect no personal data on any server, which is how we keep within India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Like any website, the web host (GitHub Pages) receives your IP address when the page loads. The fonts are stored with the app, so nothing is sent to Google. Phone count. To know how many students use the app, it adds 1 to a public counter (abacus.jasoncameron.dev) the first time it opens on a phone with internet. Only that "+1" is sent: no name, profile, progress or ID. Like any website, that counter receives your IP address for that one request. Each phone is counted once, so clearing site data may count it again.

      Your progress. Clearing the browser or changing phones deletes your streak. Save a backup file now and then, and restore it any time. ·

      Meet Jo. Jo is the little study buddy who sits on what you are reading. He gives short tips, memory keys from the topic, real-life examples and the odd friendly tease, and he cheers your quiz answers. He is written into the app, works offline and is not an AI chatbot. Tap him for a line, tap 👍 to close his cloud, or 🤫 Shh to keep him quiet for 15 minutes. To turn him off, use the Jo switch under Eye comfort on the Ask screen. He hides in focus mode.

      Permissions and the law. We have not asked for or received special permission from any university, publisher or guideline body, and none is implied. Here is what we rely on instead. Universities publish their syllabi openly for students. Facts and ideas belong to no one; only their wording is protected, so every topic is written fresh in our own words and textbooks are only cited by chapter. India's Copyright Act, 1957, Section 52, allows fair dealing for private study and research (Section 52(1)(a)) and use in the course of teaching (Section 52(1)(i)). Organisations named on the Inclusive.Si page are named with their agreement. This is our honest reading, not legal advice. If you own something here and disagree, tell us and we will fix it.

      Safety. This is a study aid for students, not medical advice for patients. Answers are written carefully from standard textbooks and guidelines, but mistakes are possible and practice changes. Before using any dose, vaccine schedule, drug, electrotherapy parameter or exercise prescription with a real patient, check it against current official guidance (MoHFW, WHO, NICE, your university syllabus) and a qualified clinician. Spotted an error? Message us on Instagram (@inclusive.si) and it will be corrected for everyone.
