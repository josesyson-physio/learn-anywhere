How answers are built. Choose your university, year, question type and marks. The answer follows the layout Indian BPT and MPT examiners expect: definition, pathophysiology, clinical features, assessment, phased management, diagram and conclusion. Advanced sections add evidence grades, outcome measures with clinically important change scores, recent trials and viva questions.

Marking guide. The marks split is a typical pattern for Indian health science university papers. Always check your own university's syllabus and previous question papers.

References. Library topics are original summaries that point to standard textbooks (see the Reference shelf in the Library) and to real guidelines and trials you can open on PubMed. No book text is copied. Always read the chapter for depth.

Free forever. No account, no fees, no ads. The library grows topic by topic and anyone can add to it on GitHub.
